What this campaign includes

Unova unlocks after a Sinnoh win in the documented build and includes Team Plasma encounters before Alder's league. The run uses the same core branching-map decisions: decide between battles, catches, items, trades, events, healing, and move improvements before committing to a path.

Search demand for this guide

Unova, Unova Battle Tower, and Unova Reddit are established search suggestions. This page consolidates that intent rather than creating several short, overlapping pages.

Preparation checklist

  • Cover the major type weaknesses represented by your current route and upcoming boss.
  • Keep the lead slot healthy and review speed before entering a mandatory fight.
  • Use healing and Move Tutor opportunities before the league rather than carrying unused resources.
  • Check the current game version before relying on fixed balance or encounter data.
