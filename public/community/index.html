How Reddit evidence is used

A repeated player question is evidence that a guide is needed. It is not automatically evidence that the most-upvoted answer is mechanically correct. Before a changing claim becomes an unqualified fact, it should be reproduced in the current game build.

01

Discover

Record repeated questions, failed runs, unclear tooltips, and conflicting answers.

02

Cross-check

Compare comments with current game behavior and versioned public implementations.

03

Publish

Label the game version, verification date, source, and any remaining uncertainty.

Current community-backed topics

  • Pokésort: daily hints, spoilers, and answer sharing.
  • Traits: same-type breakpoints, shiny contribution, and defensive trade-offs.
  • Battle Tower: trait planning, held-item choices, and repeatable team strength.
  • Nuzlocke: risk control and team recommendations that prioritize survival.
Editorial rule

Reddit wording is summarized, not copied. Community claims that have not been reproduced are labeled as community advice instead of official mechanics.
