Team synergy

Pokelike Traits Explained

Type traits are a core Battle Tower system: stacking same-type team members unlocks tiered bonuses, while shiny members contribute additional trait value.

COMMUNITY-SOURCED

This strategy section incorporates the recurring team-building discussion in the r/Pokelike Tips and Tricks thread. Mechanics that can change remain labeled rather than converted into unsupported percentages.

How traits affect team building

Community discussion focuses on the first meaningful breakpoint: two Pokémon sharing a type can activate a Tier 1 bonus. That makes a catch or trade valuable for more than its individual stats. Shiny Pokémon also contribute extra trait value in the documented Tower rules, so a shiny can change which breakpoint your team reaches.

01

Count shared types

Check both types on every team member. A dual-type Pokémon may advance two different synergies at once.

02

Price the breakpoint

Do not replace a good counter merely to light up a trait. Compare the new passive bonus with the coverage and speed you lose.

03

Scout the enemy theme

Enemy teams can have traits too. Use the Tower preview to avoid stacking into a matchup that directly punishes your plan.

Community-backed planning rules

  • Build one reliable core first. Reaching a useful low-tier synergy is safer than chasing several incomplete traits.
  • Shiny value is strategic. Evaluate the extra trait contribution before replacing a shiny with a small raw-stat upgrade.
  • Keep an off-type answer. Monotype stacking can amplify one shared weakness; retain a lead or switch-in that covers it.
  • Recalculate after every catch and trade. Dual typings can move more than one trait threshold.

What still needs live verification

Exact tier thresholds, effect percentages, and balance changes are not filled from memory. The next database release should record the game version, threshold, effect, and last-tested date for every trait.
