First run

Pokelike Beginner Guide

Pokelike is a browser-based Pokémon roguelike built around irreversible route choices, team management, eight gyms, and a final league.

The run in one minute

Choose a generation, select Normal or Nuzlocke, and pick a starter. Each map is a branching series of reachable nodes. Battles, catches, items, trades, events, Pokémon Centers, and Move Tutors compete for your route; each map ends with a Gym Leader, and the final map leads to the Elite Four and Champion.

Normal or Nuzlocke?

Normal

Designed for learning. Depending on the chosen wipe rule, a full-team faint can restart the area or end the run.

Nuzlocke

Hardcore mode. A fainted team member is normally lost permanently, and a full wipe ends the run.

Five decisions that matter

  1. Read the next layers. A reachable node closes alternatives on that layer, so look ahead before clicking.
  2. Protect team coverage. Catching raw power is less useful if several members share the same weakness.
  3. Plan healing. A Pokémon Center can restore the team, but the route needed to reach it has an opportunity cost.
  4. Respect speed and immunities. Faster Pokémon act first; an immune target takes no damage from that move type.
  5. Prepare before the boss. Gym and league teams are the tests that determine progression.
